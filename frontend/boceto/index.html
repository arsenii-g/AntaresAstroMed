THE LOOP

Sense. Evaluate. Act.

01

Sense

Scan your card and a small device reads pulse, body temperature, motion and push-ups, plus the cabin air around you.

02

Evaluate

Simple rules, each one explained on screen. A warning only rises when readings stay high, so one bad sample never calls Earth.

03

Act

What to do right now, a trend line for each measure once it has 7 readings, and a summary ready for the flight surgeon.
