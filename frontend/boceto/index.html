THE LOOP

Sense. Evaluate. Act.

01

Sense

A small device on the crew member reads pulse, body temperature and motion, plus the cabin air around them. Nothing to press: it just runs.

02

Evaluate

Simple rules, each one explained on screen. A warning only rises when readings stay high, so one bad sample never calls Earth.

03

Act

What to do right now, how each indicator is trending over the week, and a summary ready for the flight surgeon.
